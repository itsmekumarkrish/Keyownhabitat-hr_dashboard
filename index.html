Frequently Asked Questions

What is HOAS?

HOAS (Home Ownership Advisory Service) is KeyOwn Habitat's dedicated advisory framework helping tenants transition into homeowners through credit readiness and developer matching.

How much does HOAS cost?

We charge a one-time advisory fee of ₹9,999 which covers your full credit assessment and personalized homeownership roadmap.

Do I need a perfect credit score to start?

No. We work with clients across all credit tiers, identifying documentation and score gaps to get you mortgage-ready.

How long does the transition take?

Depending on your current financial profile, clients typically become mortgage-ready within immediate to 3–6 months.

Are projects RERA registered?

Yes. KeyOwn operates in 100% compliance with RERA guidelines and exclusively works with RERA-approved developers and partner banks.
